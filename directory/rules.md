# Terminal Directory rules

The Terminal Directory lists sites made with Terminal Web Builder, so people can find them.
Every site in it must follow these rules. By adding a site, you agree to them.

## Not allowed

1. **Hate speech.** Attacking or demeaning people for their race, ethnicity, nationality,
   religion, disability, sex, gender identity or sexual orientation, including slurs and
   hate symbols.
2. **Harassment and threats.** Threats of violence, encouraging violence or self-harm,
   bullying, or support for terrorist or violent extremist groups.
3. **Sexual content involving minors.** Zero tolerance. Such sites are removed at once and
   reported to the authorities (in Canada, Cybertip.ca; in the US, NCMEC).
4. **Sexually explicit content.** No pornography or explicit sexual material.
5. **Illegal goods and services.** Selling or arranging drugs, weapons, stolen data or
   accounts, counterfeit money or documents, or hacking for hire.
6. **Scams and harmful software.** Phishing, fake giveaways, "money doubling", or links to
   viruses and other malware.
7. **Other people's private information.** Home addresses, phone numbers, ID numbers or
   other personal details of someone without their permission.
8. **Pirated material.** Hosting or linking to copies of films, music, games, software or
   books that you don't have the right to share.

## How the rules are kept

- **Every new site is checked automatically** before it is listed. A site that looks like
  it breaks a rule is held back for a person to review instead of being listed.
- **Listed sites are read again every day.** A site that adds content against the rules
  is hidden automatically until it is reviewed.
- **Anyone can report a site** with the "Report a site" form. A person reads every report.
- A site that breaks the rules can be **held, removed, or removed and blocked** from being
  added again. We may contact you on your sign-up page on GitHub first.

## Questions and appeals

If your site was held or removed and you think that was a mistake, reply on your sign-up
page on GitHub, or email TerminalWeb@protonmail.com.
